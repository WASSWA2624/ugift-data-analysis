# Lwatama HC III — state of the toolkit transcription

The team verified this facility on the paper toolkit and photographed the filled
pages. `Asset-Verification-Toolkit.docx` has now been **partially** transcribed
from those photographs. The discussion guide (questions 1a–4) was already
transcribed and matches `135_…discussion-guide_ref0616.jpg`.

`transcription-review.csv` in this folder is the working sheet: one row per
schedule item, showing what was entered, what is only a candidate reading, and
what has no photograph at all.

## What was entered

Items 1–8 (B.P. Machine, Digital → Drip Stand) from
`138_…checklist-opening_ref0613.jpg`, plus the Wheel Chair note from
`136_…checklist-tagged-lwatama_ref0609.jpg`.

That page is the only one photographed squarely enough to read at cell level.
Even there, the ink columns (Department, Asset Number, Item Description, Tag
Number, Date Placed In Service) are readable while the **Equipment status and
Remarks columns are in pencil** and mostly are not. Those cells were left blank
rather than guessed, so several entered rows carry an asset but no status.

The Wheel Chair note is written in ink across the row and reads clearly:
*"Wheel chairs available but not included on the copy/list of the UgIFT
program."* The row is also marked "Not received".

## What was not entered, and why

Photographs `136`, `137` and `139` are out of focus at cell level — each frames a
**two-page spread** at ≤1280 px, giving one page roughly 600 px across fifteen
columns. Item names (pre-printed) are readable; the handwritten asset numbers,
descriptions and pencil status/remarks are not.

Candidate readings for those 25 items are recorded in the review sheet marked
`approx`, so a reviewer with the paper in hand can confirm or correct each one
quickly. They were deliberately kept out of the register: a wrong asset number
or a wrong "Functional" status is a material misstatement that nobody downstream
can detect, whereas a blank cell is visibly incomplete.

One reading needs resolving on paper before it is entered: the **Counting
chamber Neubauer** row carries a description that reads as a nebulizer
("Lintemed Nebulizer, 1 used and another placed"). Either the reading is wrong
or the team wrote on the wrong line.

## Scope caveat from the discussion guide

The N.B. at the foot of `135_…discussion-guide_ref0616.jpg` records that the
Incharge supplied a list of assets received on arrival, and that **anything
present but not on that list was not counted as a UgIFT asset**. The schedule
therefore reflects that list, not everything physically at the facility.
