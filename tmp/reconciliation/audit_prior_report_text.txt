How this was worked out
  The facility list is SCHOOLS BY DISTRICT AND HEALTH CENTRES.docx: its schools table and its list of HC III upgrades
and new constructions, plus the three regional blood banks named in the allocation. Each facility is given the team and local
government from team-distributions.docx. Entries listed twice (Kapedo phases II and III, Kidubuli HC II, the two Nyamarunda
rows) are counted once.
  Team members, supervisors and contacts are as written in team-distributions.docx. Kalangala has no team number there; it
sits with Buvuma under "Islands", so it is shown with Team 31.
  A facility counts as received when a folder of its own in raw-data-grouped holds at least one file. The folder may be a full
verification or a partial return; this report does not judge completeness.
  The list names many Phase II and III schools by sub-county, not by school. Those were linked to a team folder only where the
team's own document for that school names the sub-county (for example Wol for Awor SSS in Agago, Gimara for Gopele SSS in
Obongi, Kuluba for Nyakaliso SSS in Koboko). Spelling variants were linked by hand (Jagusi/Jaguzi, Kitwala/Kitwara,
Kasasila/Kasasira and others).
  Register only rows come from the Team 7 schools sheet, Team 19's Mitooma and Sheema lists, the Busoga seed-school
register and the DEPAUL consolidated register for Bunyoro, Tooro and Greater Mityana. The DEPAUL register labels some
districts differently from the list (Kabahango under Kamwenge, Kabambiro under Bunyangabu, Buseregenyu under Mityana); the
facility name was matched, and Kabingo was taken as the Kamwenge facility. It also carries rows for Muggi HC III labelled
Kagadi; Muggi is a Mayuge facility with its own Team 16 folder, so those rows are not counted for Kagadi.
  Sheets that only reproduce the programme list or a district budget (Team 21 "TEAM 21 SSS (2).xlsx", Team 20 "TEAM 20
SSSs.xls" apart from St Anthony SSS) and district asset records such as the Busia LG forms were not taken as evidence that a
facility sent data.
  "Possibly" notes point to a team folder in the same local government and of the same type that the list does not name. They
are leads to confirm with the team, not matches.
