FACILITY REPORT — #4
Assets Verified / Condition Summary (Good / Fair / Poor)
Most of the assets provided to the school are still in excellent conditions as the handover happened last month.
In case of any damages to a property or asset, the contractor is liable as there is a one-year guarantee in case of damages.
All assets have the name of the school engraved but not UGIFT initials.
Successes
The Asset verification at the school was a success.
The deputy teacher was very welcoming and assisted in the carrying out of the whole verification process.
Challenges
Accessing the school was difficult due to poor state of the road and the school is way up on the mountains.
The school lacks the fence putting all the school equipments at risk of theft.
Staff quarters are not enough forcing Head teachers to stay outside the school to provide more space to accommodate other teachers.
The school lacks water tanks both in school and teachers quarters.
The computers are very few according to the high number of learners.
Recommendations
The fence is required for the safety of all the school assets.
Electricity needs to be connected in the teachers quarters .
